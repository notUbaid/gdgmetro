Ek Safar (એક સફર) — One City, One Journey | Multimodal Transit Platform

The unified Ahmedabad-Gandhinagar multimodal transit platform uniting Gujarat Metro Rail (GMRC), Janmarg BRTS dedicated busways, and AMTS municipal city feeder buses into a single integrated journey planner with unified ticketing (15% rebate) and Hare-Niemeyer exact-paise fare clearinghouse.

PS-08 Problem Statement: One City, One Journey

Ek Safar solves fragmented urban mobility across the Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar agglomeration. Instead of purchasing separate tokens for the metro and cash tickets for buses, commuters use a single QR ticket with automatic 15% multimodal transfer rebates.

  • Gujarat Metro Rail Corporation (GMRC): 53 operational stations across East-West (Blue), North-South (Red), Gandhinagar (Green), and GIFT City (Purple) lines.
  • Janmarg BRTS: 14 dedicated busway corridors along 132ft Ring Road, Ashram Road, and SG Highway arterials.
  • AMTS Municipal Feeders: First-and-last-mile connectivity linking residential colonies, universities, and industrial sectors to rapid transit spines.
